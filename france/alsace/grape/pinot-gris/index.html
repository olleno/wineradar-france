    Pinot Gris in Alsace

    5 producers in Alsace make wine from Pinot Gris that has been rated, and for 2 of them it is the leading grape. The villages with the most producers are Ribeauvillé (1), Turckheim (1), Bergholtz (1), Riquewihr (1), Orschwiller (1).

    Pinot Gris gives rich, full-bodied white wines in Alsace, often with a hint of smoke. It is grown in many of the grands crus, and in warm years it is also made into late-harvest sweet wines.
