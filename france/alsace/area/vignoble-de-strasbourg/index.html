    Wine area · Vignoble de Strasbourg

    Vignoble de Strasbourg wineries

    A few kilometres west of Strasbourg the Route des Vins begins at Marlenheim, in the Couronne d’Or, and runs south through the vineyards of the Kochersberg and around Molsheim. Vines mix with fields and orchards, and from the Scharrach hill there is a view over the plain to Strasbourg cathedral. Grands crus here include Altenberg de Bergbieten, Engelberg, Steinklotz and Bruderthal, and the Bruche canal cycle path connects the vineyards with the city.
