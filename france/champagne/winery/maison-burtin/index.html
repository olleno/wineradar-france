    Maison Burtin

    Maison Burtin is a wine producer in Épernay, in the Vallée de la Marne wine area of Champagne (Montagne de Reims, Vallée de la Marne, Côte des Blancs, Côte des Bar). Its rated wines are sparkling, led by Chardonnay.
