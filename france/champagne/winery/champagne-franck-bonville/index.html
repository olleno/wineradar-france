    Champagne Franck Bonville

    Champagne Franck Bonville is a wine producer in Avize, in the Côte des Blancs wine area of Champagne (Montagne de Reims, Vallée de la Marne, Côte des Blancs, Côte des Bar). The estate farms about 15 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are sparkling, led by Chardonnay.
