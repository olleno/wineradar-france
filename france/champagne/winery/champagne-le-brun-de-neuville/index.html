    Champagne Le Brun de Neuville

    Champagne Le Brun de Neuville is a wine producer in Bethon, in the Côte des Blancs wine area of Champagne (Montagne de Reims, Vallée de la Marne, Côte des Blancs, Côte des Bar). The estate farms about 152 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are sparkling, led by Chardonnay.
