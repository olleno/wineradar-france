    Champagne Vranken

    Champagne Vranken is a wine producer in Reims, in the Montagne de Reims wine area of Champagne (Montagne de Reims, Vallée de la Marne, Côte des Blancs, Côte des Bar). Its rated wines are mostly sparkling, led by Chardonnay. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
