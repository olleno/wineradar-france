    Hautvillers · Vallée de la Marne

    Champagne J. M. Gobillard Fils

    Champagne J. M. Gobillard Fils is a wine producer in Hautvillers, in the Vallée de la Marne wine area of Champagne (Montagne de Reims, Vallée de la Marne, Côte des Blancs, Côte des Bar). The estate farms about 30 hectares of vineyard. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
