    Domaine Agnes Paquet

    Domaine Agnes Paquet is a wine producer in Meloisey, in the Côte de Beaune wine area of Burgundy (Côte d’Or, Chablis, Côte Chalonnaise, Mâconnais, Châtillonnais). Its rated wines are red and white, led by Chardonnay and Pinot Noir. Wine can be bought directly at the estate.
