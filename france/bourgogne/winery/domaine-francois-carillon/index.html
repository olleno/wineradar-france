    Domaine François Carillon

    Domaine François Carillon is a wine producer in Puligny-Montrachet, in the Côte de Beaune wine area of Burgundy (Côte d’Or, Chablis, Côte Chalonnaise, Mâconnais, Châtillonnais). The estate farms about 16 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red and white, led by Pinot Noir and Chardonnay.
