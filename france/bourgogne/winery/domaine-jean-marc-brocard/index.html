    Préhy · Chablis & Grand Auxerrois

    Domaine Jean-Marc Brocard

    Domaine Jean-Marc Brocard is a wine producer in Préhy, in the Chablis wine area of Burgundy (Côte d’Or, Chablis, Côte Chalonnaise, Mâconnais, Châtillonnais). The vineyards are converting to organic farming. Its rated wines are white, led by Chardonnay. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
