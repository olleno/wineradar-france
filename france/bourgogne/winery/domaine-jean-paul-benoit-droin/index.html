    Domaine Jean-Paul & Benoît Droin

    Domaine Jean-Paul & Benoît Droin is a wine producer in Chablis, in the Chablis wine area of Burgundy (Côte d’Or, Chablis, Côte Chalonnaise, Mâconnais, Châtillonnais). The estate farms about 26 hectares of vineyard.
