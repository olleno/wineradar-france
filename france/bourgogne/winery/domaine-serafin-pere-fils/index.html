    Domaine Sérafin Père & Fils

    Domaine Sérafin Père & Fils is a wine producer in Gevrey-Chambertin, in the Côte de Nuits wine area of Burgundy (Côte d’Or, Chablis, Côte Chalonnaise, Mâconnais, Châtillonnais). The estate farms about 5.5 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Pinot Noir.
