    Domaine Vincent & François Jouard

    Domaine Vincent & François Jouard is a wine producer in Chassagne-Montrachet, in the Côte de Beaune wine area of Burgundy (Côte d’Or, Chablis, Côte Chalonnaise, Mâconnais, Châtillonnais). The estate farms about 11 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are mostly white, led by Chardonnay and Pinot Noir.
