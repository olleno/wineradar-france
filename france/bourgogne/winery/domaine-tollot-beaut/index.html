    Domaine Tollot-Beaut

    Domaine Tollot-Beaut is a wine producer in Chorey-lès-Beaune, in the Côte de Beaune wine area of Burgundy (Côte d’Or, Chablis, Côte Chalonnaise, Mâconnais, Châtillonnais). Its rated wines are red, led by Pinot Noir.
