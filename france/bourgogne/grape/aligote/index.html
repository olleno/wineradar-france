    Aligoté in Burgundy

    10 producers in Burgundy make Aligoté that has been rated, and for 2 of them it is the leading grape. The villages with the most producers are Meursault (3), Morey-Saint-Denis (1), Rully (1), Puligny-Montrachet (1), Sampigny-lès-Maranges (1).

    Aligoté is Burgundy’s other white grape, about 6% of the vineyards (BIVB). Most of it becomes Bourgogne Aligoté, and the village of Bouzeron in the Côte Chalonnaise has its own appellation for it.
