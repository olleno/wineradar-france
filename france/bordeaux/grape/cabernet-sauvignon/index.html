    Cabernet Sauvignon in Bordeaux

    100 producers in Bordeaux make Cabernet Sauvignon that has been rated, and for 59 of them it is the leading grape. The villages with the most producers are Saint-Émilion (21), Pomerol (16), Pauillac (10), Libourne (9), Saint-Julien-Beychevelle (6).

    Cabernet Sauvignon is Bordeaux’s second red grape, about a fifth of the red vines (CIVB). It is at its most important on the gravel of the Médoc and the Graves, where it gives the backbone of the great left-bank wines.
