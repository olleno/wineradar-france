    Wineries in Sauternes

    Sauternes has 9 wine producers in our guide, in the Graves area. 4 of them welcome visitors, and 1 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Sémillon and Merlot. Within walking distance there are 7 places to eat and drink and 1 places to stay.
