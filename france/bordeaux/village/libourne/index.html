    Wineries in Libourne

    Libourne has 31 wine producers in our guide, in the Libournais area. 3 of them welcome visitors, and 1 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Merlot and Cabernet Sauvignon. Within walking distance there are 28 places to eat and drink and 4 places to stay.
