    Château Le Pin

    Château Le Pin is a wine producer in Pomerol, in the Libournais wine area of Bordeaux (Médoc, Graves, Sauternes, Saint-Émilion, Pomerol, Entre-deux-Mers, Blaye, Bourg). The estate farms about 3 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Merlot.
