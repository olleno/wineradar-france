    Château Haut-Bacalan

    Château Haut-Bacalan is a wine producer in Pessac, in the Graves wine area of Bordeaux (Médoc, Graves, Sauternes, Saint-Émilion, Pomerol, Entre-deux-Mers, Blaye, Bourg). The estate farms about 8 hectares of vineyard. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
