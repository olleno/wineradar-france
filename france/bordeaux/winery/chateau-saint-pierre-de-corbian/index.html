    Château Saint Pierre De Corbian

    Château Saint Pierre De Corbian is a wine producer in Saint-Seurin-de-Cadourne, in the Médoc wine area of Bordeaux (Médoc, Graves, Sauternes, Saint-Émilion, Pomerol, Entre-deux-Mers, Blaye, Bourg). The estate farms about 3.9 hectares of vineyard. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
