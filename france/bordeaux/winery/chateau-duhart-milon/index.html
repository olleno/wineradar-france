    Château Duhart-Milon

    Château Duhart-Milon is a wine producer in Pauillac, in the Médoc wine area of Bordeaux (Médoc, Graves, Sauternes, Saint-Émilion, Pomerol, Entre-deux-Mers, Blaye, Bourg). The estate farms about 71 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are mostly red, led by Cabernet Sauvignon and Sémillon. The producer welcomes visitors.
