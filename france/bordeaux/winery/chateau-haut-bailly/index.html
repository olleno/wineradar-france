    Château Haut-Bailly

    Château Haut-Bailly is a wine producer in Léognan, in the Graves wine area of Bordeaux (Médoc, Graves, Sauternes, Saint-Émilion, Pomerol, Entre-deux-Mers, Blaye, Bourg). The estate farms about 30 hectares of vineyard. The producer welcomes visitors.
