    Timorasso in Piedmont

    13 producers in Piedmont make Timorasso that has been rated, and for 6 of them it is the leading grape. The villages with the most producers are Tortona (3), Nizza Monferrato (2), Monleale (1), San Martino Alfieri (1), Costigliole d'Asti (1).
