    Monteu Roero · Roero

    Angelo Negro

    Angelo Negro is a wine producer in Monteu Roero, in the Roero wine area of Piedmont (Langhe, Roero, Monferrato, Gavi, Alto Piemonte, Canavese). The estate farms about 70 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are mostly red, led by Nebbiolo, Arneis and Barbera. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
