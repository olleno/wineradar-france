    Corneliano d'Alba · Roero

    Valdinera

    Valdinera is a wine producer in Corneliano d'Alba, in the Roero wine area of Piedmont (Langhe, Roero, Monferrato, Gavi, Alto Piemonte, Canavese). Its rated wines are mostly white, led by Arneis and Nebbiolo. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
