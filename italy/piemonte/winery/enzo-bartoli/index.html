    Enzo Bartoli

    Enzo Bartoli is a wine producer in Priocca, in the Roero wine area of Piedmont (Langhe, Roero, Monferrato, Gavi, Alto Piemonte, Canavese). Its rated wines are mostly red, led by Barbera.
