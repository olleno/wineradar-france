    Scagliola Sansì

    Scagliola Sansì is a wine producer in Calosso, in the Monferrato wine area of Piedmont (Langhe, Roero, Monferrato, Gavi, Alto Piemonte, Canavese). Its rated wines are mostly sparkling, led by Moscato and Barbera. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
