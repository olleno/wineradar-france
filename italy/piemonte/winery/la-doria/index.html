    La Doria

    La Doria is a wine producer in Cossano Belbo, in the Langhe wine area of Piedmont (Langhe, Roero, Monferrato, Gavi, Alto Piemonte, Canavese). Its rated wines are white, led by Cortese. The producer welcomes visitors.
