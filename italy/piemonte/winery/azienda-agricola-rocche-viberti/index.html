    Azienda Agricola Rocche Viberti

    Azienda Agricola Rocche Viberti is a wine producer in Castiglione Falletto, in the Langhe wine area of Piedmont (Langhe, Roero, Monferrato, Gavi, Alto Piemonte, Canavese). The estate farms about 4.5 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Nebbiolo. The producer welcomes visitors.
