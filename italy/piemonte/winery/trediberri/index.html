    Trediberri

    Trediberri is a wine producer in La Morra, in the Langhe wine area of Piedmont (Langhe, Roero, Monferrato, Gavi, Alto Piemonte, Canavese). The estate farms about 11 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Nebbiolo and Barbera.
