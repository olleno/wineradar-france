    Wineries in Serralunga d'Alba

    Serralunga d'Alba has 19 wine producers in our guide, in the Langhe area. 12 of them welcome visitors, and 3 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Nebbiolo, Pinot Noir, Barbera and Chardonnay. Within walking distance there are 5 places to eat and drink and 1 places to stay.
