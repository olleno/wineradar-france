    Wineries in Alba

    Alba has 16 wine producers in our guide, in the Langhe area. 11 of them welcome visitors, and 1 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Nebbiolo, Barbera, Moscato and Pinot Noir. Within walking distance there are 30 places to eat and drink and 12 places to stay.
