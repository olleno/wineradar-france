    Wineries in Barolo

    Barolo has 22 wine producers in our guide, in the Langhe area. 12 of them welcome visitors, and 6 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Nebbiolo, Barbera, Nascetta and Cortese. Within walking distance there are 12 places to eat and drink and 9 places to stay.
