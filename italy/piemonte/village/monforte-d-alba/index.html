    Wineries in Monforte d'Alba

    Monforte d'Alba has 39 wine producers in our guide, in the Langhe area. 19 of them welcome visitors, and 7 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Nebbiolo, Barbera, Chardonnay and Arneis. Within walking distance there are 6 places to eat and drink and 3 places to stay.
