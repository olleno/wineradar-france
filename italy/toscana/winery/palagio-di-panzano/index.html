    Greve in Chianti · Chianti Classico

    Palagio di Panzano

    Palagio di Panzano is a wine producer in Greve in Chianti, in the Chianti Classico wine area of Tuscany (Chianti Classico, Montalcino, Montepulciano, San Gimignano, Bolgheri, Maremma, Chianti). The estate farms about 7.5 hectares of vineyard, organically. Its rated wines are red, led by Sangiovese. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
