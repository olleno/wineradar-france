    Fosdinovo · Chianti & northern Tuscany

    Terenzuola

    Terenzuola is a wine producer in Fosdinovo, in the Chianti wine area of Tuscany (Chianti Classico, Montalcino, Montepulciano, San Gimignano, Bolgheri, Maremma, Chianti). The estate farms about 22 hectares of vineyard, organically. Its rated wines are mostly white, led by Vermentino, Vermentino nero and Canaiolo. Wine can be bought directly at the estate.
