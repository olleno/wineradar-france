    La Lastra

    La Lastra is a wine producer in San Gimignano, in the San Gimignano wine area of Tuscany (Chianti Classico, Montalcino, Montepulciano, San Gimignano, Bolgheri, Maremma, Chianti). Its rated wines are mostly white, led by Vernaccia di San Gimignano, Canaiolo and Sangiovese.
