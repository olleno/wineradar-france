    Montespertoli · Chianti & northern Tuscany

    Fattoria Poggio Capponi

    Fattoria Poggio Capponi is a wine producer in Montespertoli, in the Chianti wine area of Tuscany (Chianti Classico, Montalcino, Montepulciano, San Gimignano, Bolgheri, Maremma, Chianti). The estate farms about 35 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are mostly red, led by Sangiovese, Syrah and Chardonnay. Wine can be bought directly at the estate.
