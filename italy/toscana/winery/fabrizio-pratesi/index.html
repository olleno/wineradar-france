    Fabrizio Pratesi

    Fabrizio Pratesi is a wine producer in Carmignano, in the Chianti wine area of Tuscany (Chianti Classico, Montalcino, Montepulciano, San Gimignano, Bolgheri, Maremma, Chianti). The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
