    Greve in Chianti · Chianti Classico

    Podere Poggio Scalette

    Podere Poggio Scalette is a wine producer in Greve in Chianti, in the Chianti Classico wine area of Tuscany (Chianti Classico, Montalcino, Montepulciano, San Gimignano, Bolgheri, Maremma, Chianti). The estate farms about 14 hectares of vineyard. Its rated wines are red, led by Sangiovese and Merlot. The producer welcomes visitors and sells wine directly from the estate.
