    Fattoria Aldobrandesca

    Fattoria Aldobrandesca is a wine producer in Sorano, in the Maremma wine area of Tuscany (Chianti Classico, Montalcino, Montepulciano, San Gimignano, Bolgheri, Maremma, Chianti). Its rated wines are mostly red, led by Malbec and Aleatico.
