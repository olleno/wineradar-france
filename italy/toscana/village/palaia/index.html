    Wineries in Palaia

    Palaia has 4 wine producers in our guide, in the Chianti area. 1 of them welcome visitors, and 2 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Sangiovese, Cabernet Franc, Syrah and Petit Verdot. Within walking distance there are 7 places to eat and drink and 8 places to stay.
