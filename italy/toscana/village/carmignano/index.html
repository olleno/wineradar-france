    Wineries in Carmignano

    Carmignano has 8 wine producers in our guide, in the Chianti area. 4 of them welcome visitors, and 2 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Sangiovese, Trebbiano, Vermentino and Merlot. Within walking distance there are 11 places to eat and drink and 4 places to stay.
