    Wineries in Castagneto Carducci

    Castagneto Carducci has 32 wine producers in our guide, in the Bolgheri area. 11 of them welcome visitors, and 1 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Cabernet Franc, Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Vermentino. Within walking distance there are 12 places to eat and drink and 6 places to stay.
