    Wineries in San Gimignano

    San Gimignano has 20 wine producers in our guide, in the San Gimignano area. 5 of them welcome visitors, and 3 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Vernaccia di San Gimignano, Sangiovese, Canaiolo and Merlot. Within walking distance there are 23 places to eat and drink and 15 places to stay.
