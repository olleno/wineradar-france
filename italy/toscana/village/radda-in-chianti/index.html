    Wineries in Radda in Chianti

    Radda in Chianti has 28 wine producers in our guide, in the Chianti Classico area. 3 of them welcome visitors, and 1 farm organically or biodynamically. The grapes you meet most often in its rated wines are Sangiovese, Cabernet Sauvignon and Chardonnay. Within walking distance there are 8 places to eat and drink and 10 places to stay.
